Disease Name: Food Poisoning (Gastroenteritis)

Symptoms:
- Nausea and vomiting
- Watery or bloody diarrhea
- Abdominal pain and stomach cramps
- Low-grade fever
- General weakness

Description: An illness caused by eating contaminated, spoiled, or toxic food. Symptoms typically begin within a few hours of eating the contaminated food and are localized to the digestive tract.
