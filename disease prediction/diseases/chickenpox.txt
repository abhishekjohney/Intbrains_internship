Disease Name: Chickenpox (Varicella)

Symptoms:
- An itchy, blister-like rash all over the body
- Fever
- Fatigue and a general feeling of being unwell (malaise)
- Loss of appetite
- Headache

Description: A highly contagious viral infection causing an itchy, blister-like rash on the skin. It is most common in children but can affect adults who haven't had it or haven't been vaccinated.
