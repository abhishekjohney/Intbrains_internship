Disease Name: Migraine

Symptoms:
- Severe throbbing pain or pulsing sensation, usually on one side of the head
- Extreme sensitivity to light, sound, and sometimes smell
- Nausea and vomiting
- Visual auras (seeing flashes of light or blind spots)
- Dizziness or blurred vision

Description: A neurological condition characterized by intense, debilitating headaches, often accompanied by sensory warning signs and extreme physical sensitivity to the environment.
