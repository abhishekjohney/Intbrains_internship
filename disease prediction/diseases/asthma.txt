Disease Name: Asthma

Symptoms:
- Shortness of breath
- Chest tightness or pain
- Wheezing when exhaling
- Trouble sleeping caused by shortness of breath or coughing
- Coughing or wheezing attacks worsened by a respiratory virus

Description: A chronic condition in which your airways narrow and swell, and may produce extra mucus. This can make breathing difficult and trigger coughing and a whistling sound (wheezing) when you breathe out.
