Disease Name: Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD)

Symptoms:
- Heartburn (a burning sensation in your chest, usually after eating)
- Regurgitation of food or sour liquid (acid reflux)
- Upper abdominal or chest pain
- Difficulty swallowing (dysphagia)
- Sensation of a lump in your throat
- Chronic cough or laryngitis

Description: A digestive disorder that occurs when stomach acid or bile flows back into your food pipe (esophagus). The constant backwash of acid irritates the lining of your esophagus.
