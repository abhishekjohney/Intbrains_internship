Disease Name: Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)

Symptoms:
- Often completely asymptomatic (known as the "silent killer")
- Severe headaches (in extremely high, dangerous cases)
- Shortness of breath
- Nosebleeds
- Dizziness or blurred vision

Description: A common condition in which the long-term force of the blood against your artery walls is high enough that it may eventually cause health problems, such as heart disease.
