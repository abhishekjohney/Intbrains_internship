Disease Name: Osteoarthritis

Symptoms:
- Joint pain during or after movement
- Joint stiffness, especially upon waking up or after being inactive
- Joint tenderness when applying light pressure
- Loss of flexibility and range of motion
- Grating sensation or popping crackles when using the joint
- Bone spurs (extra bits of bone) around the affected joint

Description: The most common form of arthritis, occurring when the protective cartilage that cushions the ends of your bones wears down over time. It most commonly affects joints in your hands, knees, hips, and spine.
