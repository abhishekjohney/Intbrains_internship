Sachin Tendulkar is an Indian former international cricketer widely regarded as one of the greatest cricketers of all time. He holds several world records, including being the all-time highest run-scorer in international cricket and the only batsman to score 100 international centuries. Tendulkar played for India for over 24 years and was a part of the team that won the 2011 Cricket World Cup. He received numerous awards, including the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, and was inducted into the ICC Cricket Hall of Fame in 2019. Born on April 24, 1973, Tendulkar began playing cricket at age 11 and was coached by Ramakant Achrekar at Shivaji Park in Dadar. He played for Sharadashram School and later for the Cricket Club of India, before making his debut as a cricketer for India on November 15, 1989. Throughout his career, Tendulkar received numerous accolades and is regarded as a symbol of national pride in India.
